\section{The variance}

Let \(X\) be a real-valued random variable with finite second moment
and let \(\mu=\E{X}\). We want to show that
\[
  \Var{X}=\E{X^2}-(\E{X})^2.
\]

Starting from the definition of variance, we expand the square
and use linearity of expectation:
\begin{align*}
  \operatorname{Var}(X)
  &= \E{(X-\mu)^2}
    \qquad  \bigl(\E{X}=\mu) \\
  &= \E{X^2-2\mu X+\mu^2} \\
  &= \E{X^2}-2\mu\E{X}+\E{\mu^2} \\
  &= \E{X^2}-2\mu^2+\mu^2 \\
  &= \E{X^2}-\mu^2 \\
  &= \boxed{\E{X^2}-(\E{X})^2}.
\end{align*}
