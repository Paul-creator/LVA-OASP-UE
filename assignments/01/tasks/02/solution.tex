\section{The variance}

Let \(X\) be a real-valued random variable with finite second moment
and let \(\mu=\mathbb{E}[X]\). We want to show that
\[
  \operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-(\mathbb{E}[X])^2.
\]

Starting from the definition of variance, we expand the square
and use linearity of expectation:
\begin{align*}
  \operatorname{Var}(X)
  &= \mathbb{E}[(X-\mu)^2]
    \qquad  \bigl(\mathbb{E}[X]=\mu) \\
  &= \mathbb{E}[X^2-2\mu X+\mu^2] \\
  &= \mathbb{E}[X^2]-2\mu\mathbb{E}[X]+\mathbb{E}[\mu^2] \\
  &= \mathbb{E}[X^2]-2\mu^2+\mu^2 \\
  &= \mathbb{E}[X^2]-\mu^2 \\
  &= \boxed{\mathbb{E}[X^2]-(\mathbb{E}[X])^2}.
\end{align*}
